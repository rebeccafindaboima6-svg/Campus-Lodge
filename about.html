OUR STORY

About Campus Lodge

A student accommodation website concept designed to make room information easier to find.

WHY WE EXIST

Helping students make informed choices.

Finding accommodation in a new area can be stressful. Students need to understand room types, prices, facilities, location and the booking process before making a decision.

Campus Lodge is a proposed online platform that brings these details together in one organised place. The goal is to help students explore options and contact accommodation management more conveniently.

This is a student website project. Any accommodation, facilities and prices shown are sample content until confirmed by a real provider.
